# Support

This kit is free and made by [@rahmanbuilds](https://www.rahmanbuilds.com).

**Questions, bugs, ideas, and showing off what you made: the Discord.**
https://discord.gg/6DbpJUj8GT

When you ask for help, paste:

1. What you ran (the exact command, or what you said to Claude).
2. What you expected.
3. What you got: the error text, copied, not a photo of the screen.
4. Your versions: `node --version`, `ffmpeg -version` (first line), `python --version`.

Before you ask, try asking Claude in the kit folder: **"This failed, here is the error: ..."**.
It can read the kit's code and docs and fixes most setup problems on the spot.
