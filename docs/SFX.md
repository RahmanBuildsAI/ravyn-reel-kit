# Free sound effects you are allowed to use

The kit ships four starter sounds in `studio/sfx/` that were made for it, so they are yours to use
anywhere. For more, these libraries are free. **Licences change, so read the licence on the page
you download from, every time.** When a sound needs credit, put the credit in your video
description.

| Library | Licence (check on the site) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Pixabay Sound Effects | Pixabay Content License: free for commercial use, no credit required | big, easy to search |
| Freesound | each sound has its own licence. Filter by **Creative Commons 0** to need no credit | the largest library; CC BY sounds need a credit |
| Mixkit | Mixkit Sound Effects Free License: free in your videos | small, well made |
| Zapsplat | free tier with credit, or paid without | very large |
| YouTube Audio Library | free for your videos, some need credit | inside YouTube Studio |

## What to download first

A small kit covers most reels:

1. **Two or three soft whooshes** for scene changes and the window moving.
2. **A few clicks and taps** for UI landing, list items, buttons.
3. **A typing or tick sound** for text and counters.
4. **A paper or swipe sound** for screenshots and cards sliding in.
5. **One low, soft impact** for the single biggest moment.

Put them in `studio/sfx/`. ravyn lists that folder in its Media drawer. Or keep a big library
anywhere and point ravyn at it: set `SFX_LIBRARY` to its folder before starting ravyn.

## The rules that make sound work

- One sound per visual event: a card lands, a row appears, the window moves. Never on every word.
- Your voice is always the loudest thing. Effects sit well under it.
- Rotate: if one sound plays more than about 8 times in a reel, swap in a second version.
- Silence is a tool. A beat with no effect makes the next one hit harder.
