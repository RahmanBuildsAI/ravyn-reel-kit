Where things are

    

      
Review modethe default: the picture, a scene strip, and a side panel with the scene you are on (good / not right), Claude's fixes to check (before / after), your hand edits and the notes. Clicking the picture does nothing but notes; E opens the full editor. Anything you move or retype in Edit mode is kept through Claude's rebuilds.

      
The railleft edge: Media, Text, Captions, Sound, Blocks, Scenes. One click opens a drawer, Esc or the same click closes it.

      
The pictureclick to select, drag to move, corners to resize, the round handle to rotate, double-click text to type. A small toolbar appears on what you select. Right-click for more.

      
The cardright side: the essentials of the selected clip. The arrows below open keyframes, effects, media and the rest. The diamond next to a value keys it at the playhead.

      
The timelinebottom: selection and razor tools, split, marker, fit. Everything else (snap, fold, tracks, zoom) is under "more". Right-click a clip for its menu.

      
Groupsselect clips, Ctrl+G: they become one block. Double-click the block to work inside it (the timeline shows only its tracks, the picture keeps the whole reel), Esc or the reel button to come back, Ctrl+Shift+G to ungroup. Clips keep their real times, so nothing moves.

      
Ctrl+/the command box: type what you want and press Enter.

      
NotesN (or "note" under the picture), then click the spot or drag a box, type, Enter. The pin shows around its frame and on the ruler. The Notes drawer lists them all: click to jump, double-click to edit, the tick marks one fixed. Claude reads them with node ravyn/cli.mjs notes <reel>.

      
Exporttop right: Compile, Check, Test render, Render, Board, in order. One job at a time; the Log shows it.
